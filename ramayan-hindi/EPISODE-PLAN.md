# Episode plan: every event, in Valmiki's order

From Ep 19 on, every event of Valmiki's Ramayana gets its own part (about 60 s each), not only the famous ones. Follow Valmiki first. Anything from a later telling (Tulsidas, folk tradition) is only added when it's widely known, and is noted in the README line for that episode.

Sarga numbers follow valmikiramayan.net; other editions can differ by one or two. Before writing an episode, check the sarga on valmikiramayan.net or vedapath.app.

Done so far: Ep 14-16 (Aranya Kanda end), Ep 17 Shabari (Aranya 74), Ep 18 Ram meets Hanuman (Kishkindha 2-4).

## Sundara Kanda (plan when Kishkindha is done)

The leap over the ocean, Mainaka, Surasa, Simhika, Lankini, the search of Lanka, Ashoka Vatika, Ravana's threats, Trijata's dream, the ring, the chudamani, the garden destroyed, Akshaya Kumara, Indrajit's brahmastra, Ravana's court, the burning of Lanka, the return and the honey garden, the news to Ram.
